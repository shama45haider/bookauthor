Frequently Asked Questions

What is Shalom Bayit?

Shalom Bayit (שלום בית, also pronounced Shalom Bayis) means “peace in the home.” It is the Torah ideal of a marriage built on harmony, respect, and love. Our Sages teach that when a husband and wife merit it, the Shechinah, the Divine Presence, dwells between them.

Who is this book written for?

It is written specifically for men: husbands who want to build genuine peace in their marriage, whether they have been married thirty years or thirty days. It is also a fitting sefer for a chatan (chosson) preparing for his wedding.

What is inside the book?

Thirty concise chapters, each presenting one foundation of Shalom Bayit drawn from classic Torah sources and foundational texts, and each closing with a heartfelt prayer that reinforces the lesson. Every chapter is short enough to learn in a single sitting, and bonus material is included at the end of the book.

Who has endorsed the book?

The book is endorsed by Dayanim (rabbinical judges), Poskim (halachic authorities), and rabbanim who counsel couples in Shalom Bayit, including Rabbi Dr. Shmuel Katz, Rabbi Eliyahu Ben Chaim, Rabbi Igal Haimoff, Rabbi Aharon Chein, and Rabbi Refael Ribacoff.

Does it make a good gift?

Yes. Successful Marriage is a meaningful gift for a chatan, at a sheva brachot, for an anniversary, or for any husband who wants to bring more peace into his home.
