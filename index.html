Invite the Shechinah Into Your Home

Shalom Bayit, peace in the home, is not a matter of luck or temperament. It is built, one foundation at a time.

Grounded in timeless Torah principles and foundational spiritual texts, this essential guide offers a clear roadmap for every Jewish husband who wants to cultivate genuine peace in his marriage and merit the resting of the Shechinah, the Divine Presence, within the walls of his home. Across 30 concise chapters, each closing with a heartfelt prayer, you will explore the essential foundations of Shalom Bayit.
