About the Book · שְׁלוֹם בַּיִת

Invite the Shechinah Into Your Home

Shalom Bayit, peace in the home, is not a matter of luck or temperament. It is built, one foundation at a time.

Grounded in timeless Torah principles and foundational spiritual texts, this essential guide offers a clear roadmap for every Jewish husband who wants to cultivate genuine peace in his marriage and merit the resting of the Shechinah, the Divine Presence, within the walls of his home.

Across 30 concise chapters, you will explore the essential foundations of Shalom Bayit along with the heartfelt prayers that bring them to life.

Inside This Book

  • 30 foundations and prayers to merit the resting of the Shechinah in the home
  • Short chapters you can learn in a single sitting
  • Torah sources throughout, drawn from classic and foundational texts
  • A prayer at the close of every chapter to reinforce the lesson
  • Practical guidance written specifically for men
  • Bonus material at the end of the book

Who It’s For

It is written specifically for men: husbands who want to build genuine peace in their marriage, whether they have been married thirty years or thirty days. It is also a fitting sefer for a chatan (chosson) preparing for his wedding.

It makes a meaningful gift at a sheva brachot, for an anniversary, or for any husband who wants to bring more peace into his home.
